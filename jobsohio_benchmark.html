Methodology

Data source: BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW), 2013–2025. Private sector only. Annual figures average four quarterly employment levels.

National benchmark: For each of JobsOhio’s target industries, peer states’ 2013–2025 growth rate is summarized as a median and applied to Ohio’s 2013 base to derive an expected 2025 figure. The size-adjusted view includes only states whose employment in that industry is within one-third to three times Ohio’s, correcting for the tendency of smaller bases to post higher percentage growth. The unadjusted view uses all states. Ohio lags under both; the toggle lets you see the sensitivity.

Border states (head-to-head): Ohio’s growth rate shown directly alongside each neighbor’s, with no modeling. Hover to see the absolute employment behind each rate, since a small state’s high percentage can rest on a small base.

Limitations: Correlation, not causation. The Honda EV investments announced 2022–2023 will not appear in employment data until 2025–2026.

Source: BLS QCEW, 2013–2025 · Private sector, annual average employment
