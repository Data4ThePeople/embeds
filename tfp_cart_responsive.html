Data 4 The People · The USDA Thrifty Food Plan

The basket lists 1,342 foods. About 100 of them can actually be purchased in real life.

Every circle is one food in the government's Thrifty Food Plan, the basket that sets the maximum SNAP benefit, sized by its weekly budget for a family of four. A small core of buyable shopping quantities (teal) carries almost all the money. The rest are fractional entries, most budgeted at pennies a week. Tap a tier to isolate it, or filter by food group below.

Tiering note: an item is counted as a “buyable grocery” if the plan prescribes at least 2 oz per week — roughly the smallest amount that maps to how food is actually packaged and used in a week. Everything below 2 oz/week is counted as a fraction. The split is robust to where the line is drawn: at 1, 2, or 3 oz/week the buyable-grocery tier holds 90%, 83%, and 79% of the basket’s dollars respectively.
